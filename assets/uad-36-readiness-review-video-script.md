# UAD 3.6 Readiness Review — Product Walkthrough Script

## Video purpose

Show how an appraiser uses CoAppraiser to explain a potential UAD 3.6 issue and create a checklist of items to inspect before delivery.

Suggested length: 75–105 seconds.

## Core promise

Translate confusing issue text into plain-English review steps without claiming official UAD validation or acceptance.

## Screen-by-screen walkthrough

### 1. Open the readiness review

**Screen:** Dashboard → assignment → **UAD 3.6 Readiness Review**.

**Voiceover:**

“When a UAD-related issue is difficult to interpret, open the readiness review from the assignment. This is a pre-delivery explanation tool, not an official validator.”

### 2. Paste the issue

**Screen:** Paste: “Potential inconsistency in the reported finished area field.”

**Voiceover:**

“Paste the issue text exactly as received. Include only the context you can verify from the assignment.”

### 3. Generate the explanation

**Screen:** Click **Explain issue**.

**Show:** Plain-English explanation, likely report area, potential severity, review steps, what to verify, and risk notice.

**Voiceover:**

“CoAppraiser explains what the issue may refer to, points you toward the report area to inspect, and creates practical review steps.”

### 4. Compare against the source

**Screen:** Show the report/source material beside the generated review steps.

**Voiceover:**

“Use the explanation as a review aid. Compare it against the actual report, source data, assignment instructions, and applicable requirements.”

### 5. Save the issue summary

**Screen:** Open Workfile Guardian and show the UAD issue summary artifact and verification items.

**Voiceover:**

“The issue explanation and review checklist are saved to the assignment workfile so the review process remains documented.”

## Benefits to emphasize

- Plain-English explanation of potential issue text
- Clear report area and review-step guidance
- Better preparation before delivery or revision response
- Saved UAD-related workfile note and checklist

## Compliance boundaries to show or mention

“This is UAD 3.6 readiness support and issue explanation. It is not official UAD validation, GSE approval, USPAP advice, or a guarantee of lender or AMC acceptance.”

## Suggested closing CTA

“Create your account and use the readiness review to turn an unclear issue into a concrete verification checklist.”
