# Market Evidence Pack — Product Walkthrough Script

## Video purpose

Show how an appraiser uploads an MLS CSV, reviews detected fields and descriptive statistics, and creates cautious market evidence language without having CoAppraiser choose a final adjustment.

Suggested length: 90–120 seconds.

## Core promise

Turn appraiser-provided sales data into organized, reviewable market observations and a workfile memo.

## Screen-by-screen walkthrough

### 1. Open Market Evidence Pack

**Screen:** Dashboard → assignment → **Market Evidence Pack**.

**Voiceover:**

“Market Evidence Pack starts with data you provide. It is designed to organize evidence, not to replace your market analysis.”

### 2. Upload the MLS CSV

**Screen:** Upload a CSV containing fields such as sale date, sale price, list price, and days on market. Add appraiser notes.

**Voiceover:**

“Upload an MLS export and add context about the market segment, effective date, or questions you are investigating. CoAppraiser detects common column names and identifies missing fields.”

### 3. Review the summary

**Screen:** Submit the file and show sale count, date range, price statistics, DOM, list-to-sale ratio, and detected mapping.

**Voiceover:**

“The result organizes the provided records into descriptive statistics and trend observations. Review the mapping and look for missing values or data that does not belong in the analysis.”

### 4. Read the caution flags

**Screen:** Show trend observations, caution flags, report language, and verification items.

**Voiceover:**

“The workflow deliberately uses cautious language. It may suggest that further review is warranted, but it does not tell the appraiser what final adjustment to apply.”

### 5. Save the evidence memo

**Screen:** Open the saved Market Evidence Memo in Workfile Guardian.

**Voiceover:**

“Save the memo with the original export and the verification checklist. The evidence remains connected to the assignment and the appraiser’s review notes.”

## Benefits to emphasize

- Faster organization of messy MLS exports
- Visible field mapping and missing-data warnings
- Repeatable support for market conditions analysis
- Report-ready draft language with appropriate caution
- A saved evidence memo and workfile trail

## Example language to show

“The provided sales data appears to show a pattern during the analyzed period. The appraiser should verify whether the observation applies to the subject’s market segment, property type, effective date, and selected comparable sales.”

## Compliance boundaries to show or mention

“CoAppraiser summarizes appraiser-provided evidence. It does not select final comparables, calculate a required adjustment, or determine value.”

## Suggested closing CTA

“Upload your next MLS export and organize the evidence before writing the market conditions discussion.”
