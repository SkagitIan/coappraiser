The classic 3.6 trap

Your market conditions section says "stable." Your grid has time adjustments on three comps. Under the old rules, that slid through. Under 3.6's structured data, it's a contradiction a machine will catch in seconds — and then it's a revision, a delay, and a mark against you with that client.

What it actually does

  • Reads your draft report text, issue messages, or notes and explains each potential 3.6 problem the way a colleague would, not like an error code
  • Ranks what matters: hard stops first, style points last
  • Suggests the fix — revise the narrative, add support, or document why you're right
  • Builds a punch list so you clear everything in one pass instead of three

A real run-through

  1. Paste your draft report sections, or the issue text you got back.
  2. Read the plain-English breakdown and severity ranking.
  3. Work the punch list — fix, support, or defend each item.
  4. Save the review note to the workfile and deliver with confidence.

The pre-flight check, done faster

Official UAD validation still happens at submission. The point is to walk into it with nothing left to find — and a workfile note proving you looked.
