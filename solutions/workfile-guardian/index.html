Workfile Guardian

If the board ever asks, your workfile answers.

Nobody plans for the complaint. It arrives two years after delivery, about an assignment you barely remember, and your defense is whatever made it into the workfile that week. Workfile Guardian builds that file while you work — especially the part most appraisers using AI have nowhere to put: what the AI did, and what you decided.

What gets logged, automatically
  • Every source you used — MLS exports, public records, notes
  • Every AI action: the prompt, the output, the date
  • What you relied on, revised, or rejected
  • Verification checklists with your sign-offs
  • An evidence index for the whole assignment

AI without a paper trail is a liability

Appraisers are already using ChatGPT for report language — quietly, with no record of what was generated versus what was verified. If that work is ever questioned, "I cleaned it up before signing" isn't documentation. The appraisers who'll be comfortable using AI in five years are the ones whose workfiles show their process today.

What it actually does

  • Keeps drafts clearly separated from your approved final language — the distinction that matters if anyone ever reviews your process
  • Logs each workflow run with its inputs, outputs, and your verification steps
  • Builds a source log and evidence index as a natural byproduct of the work, not a Friday-afternoon chore
  • Produces a workfile memo you can export with the assignment

A real run-through

  1. Run any CoAppraiser workflow — a revision response, an evidence pack, a readiness review.
  2. The inputs, outputs, and sources are logged to the assignment as you go.
  3. Check off what you verified; note what you changed or rejected.
  4. Export the workfile record and store it with your report. Done.

Discipline without the overhead

You already know what a defensible workfile looks like. This just makes keeping one take zero extra time.
