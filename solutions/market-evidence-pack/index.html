The support you had in your head, on paper

Most time-adjustment stips aren't wrong about the adjustment — they're right that the support never made it into the report. The analysis lived in your head and your MLS session, and neither one goes in the workfile. This closes that gap in minutes.

What it actually does

  • Takes your MLS CSV export — sale dates, prices, DOM, list-to-sale ratios — and summarizes what the data actually shows
  • Drafts descriptive trend language and exhibit content you can drop into the report after review
  • Writes a time-adjustment support memo tying your adjustment to the data behind it
  • Flags where the sample is thin or the data cuts against you, so you're never surprised by a reviewer who ran the same numbers

A real run-through

  1. Export your comp search or market area sales from the MLS.
  2. Upload the CSV with your market area notes.
  3. Review the summary, tighten the language, and check the flags.
  4. Drop the language in the report and the memo in the workfile.

Your data, your conclusion

The pack describes what your data shows — it doesn't pick the adjustment. The number on the grid is yours, now with the paper trail to back it up.
