You're in — here's your checklist

The UAD 3.6 pre-delivery checklist.

Bookmark this page or print it. Run the pass on your next report before you sign — most appraisers find two or three items the first time.

1. Narrative vs. grid consistency

  • Market conditions narrative matches the direction of any time adjustments on the grid (a "stable" narrative with time adjustments applied is the single most common 3.6 contradiction).
  • Neighborhood trends, one-unit housing trends, and market conditions sections tell the same story.
  • Condition and quality ratings are consistent between the improvements section, the grid, and any repair commentary.
  • Days on market cited in the narrative match what your comp data actually shows.

2. Support that reviewers ask for under 3.6

  • Time adjustments have data support in the workfile (trend summary, paired sales, or market index — not just judgment language).
  • Comp selection reasoning is documented, including why nearby sales were not used.
  • Adjustments outside typical ranges have a sentence of support in the report or a memo in the workfile.
  • Any use of MLS remarks or public records is reconciled where the two sources disagree.

3. Structure changes that catch old habits

  • Property data fields that were free-form in the legacy URAR are now structured — check that your software's carried-forward templates didn't leave stale values.
  • Boilerplate paragraphs written for the old form don't reference sections, exhibits, or field names that no longer exist.
  • Required commentary that used to live in addenda has a home in the new structure.

4. The ten-minute pre-delivery pass

  • Read the market conditions section, then immediately look at the grid. Any contradiction?
  • Scan every adjustment column: does each non-zero adjustment have support you could point to in 30 seconds?
  • Search your narrative for "stable," "typical," and "average" — each one should still be true of this report.
  • Check the sales history and listing history sections against the MLS one last time.

5. Workfile items to log before you sign

  • The comp search criteria and export you actually relied on.
  • Support for time adjustments and any out-of-range adjustments.
  • If AI assisted with any language: what was generated, what you changed, and what you verified.
  • Your pre-delivery review itself — a dated note that you ran this pass.
