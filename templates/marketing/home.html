The questions you're already asking.

Straight answers, because you've seen enough AI hype.

Is this going to get me in trouble with USPAP?

CoAppraiser is built around the opposite goal. It drafts and organizes; you verify, decide, and sign. Every output is logged as a draft with a verification checklist, so your workfile shows exactly what was AI-assisted and what you reviewed — which is more documentation than most offices keep today.

Does it value the property or pick comps?

No. It never produces an opinion of value, selects final comparables, or sets adjustments. It works from the data and conclusions you give it and helps you write, support, and document them.

Do I have to leave TOTAL / ClickFORMS / ACI?

No. CoAppraiser runs in your browser next to whatever you already use. Paste in a stip, a report section, or an MLS export; take the output back to your software when you've reviewed it.

What happens to my data?

Your inputs and outputs are stored with your assignment so your workfile is complete, and they're yours. We don't sell data or use your assignments to advertise.
