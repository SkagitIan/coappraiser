Who it's for

Residential fee appraisers, review appraisers, trainees, and small offices that want AI helping with the typing — without adopting new appraisal software or handing judgment to a machine.

How it works

Each skill walks the AI through one narrow task: it asks for your facts, keeps your assumptions separate from your data, and returns a draft plus a checklist of what to verify before the output touches a report. You review, edit, approve — same as you'd treat a trainee's draft.

Why start here

These five cover the most common time sinks in a residential practice. Most appraisers know within one assignment whether this pays for itself — usually somewhere around the second revision response.

Quick answers

Does it give a value opinion?

No. Skills never produce an opinion of value, pick comps, or set adjustments. That's your seat.

Can outputs go straight into a report?

They're drafts with verification checklists. Review them like anything else that goes out under your signature.

What does it run on?

Inside CoAppraiser, alongside the appraisal software you already use. Nothing to install.
